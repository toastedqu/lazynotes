# Solution

## See reality.

That's it. Just see reality.

As soon as you see reality, you are at peace.

1. Did your mind react to that sentence? Finding me arrogant? Finding it absurd, or funny? Thinking "easier said than done"? Thinking I'm ignoring everyone with severe mental issues? Thinking of the meme "just be happy"?
2. Watch it closely. Who's saying all these shits in your mind? Did "you" "choose" to react this way, or did the thoughts just emerge? If so, where did they emerge from?
3. What do the thoughts sound like? Did your mind paint an image? What does it look like?
4. What do the thoughts feel like? Did irritation arise? How much? Where in your body? What does it feel like, physically?
5. Is the feeling still here? Has it shifted while you were reading these questions?
6. **This, is reality.**

As soon as you see reality, you are at peace.

&nbsp;

### Peace ≠ Painless
Let "pain" stand for every negative experience in the mind.

**"Peace" does NOT mean "painless."** Absolutely not. Here's why:
1. Your mind is a bullshit machine, fostered by computation through millennia of evolution, optimized for perceived survival.
2. When pain arises, pain is here. This is reality.
3. Whatever bullshit your mind adds on top of the pain - that occurrence is also reality.
4. If you overthink, whatever bullshit your mind adds on top of the previous bullshit - that occurrence is also reality.
5. "Peace" means: **"The pain is here. Ok. My mind is adding these and those bullshits on top. Ok."**
6. Watch the difference: **the occurrence of the bullshit IS real. The content of the bullshit IS NOT.** The content is built from human concepts, and concepts are empty.
    - Give a fuck about the content, and you are trapped in the illusion.
    - See only the occurrence, and you see reality.

As soon as you see reality, you are at peace.

This applies to the occurrence of every thought & feeling.

As soon as you see reality, you are at peace.

### The Ego Trap
You just read "see reality", and your model already converted it into a desire.

A new item appears on the character's checklist: *achieve peace*. *Become enlightened*. *See reality better than everyone else*.

Watch what happened:
1. The self heard about its own nonexistence.
2. The self turned that into a self-improvement project.
3. The self now wants to be the one who has no self.

The cure got eaten by the disease.

You cannot "achieve" seeing. Achieving is desire, and desire is the model demanding a favorable state. Seeing is not a state. Seeing is the demand dropping.

There is nothing to attain. There is nowhere to arrive. The moment you make peace a destination, you have left it.

As soon as you see reality, you are at peace.

### Who Sees?
*"Ok. I will watch my thoughts."*

Who is "I"?

Look for the watcher, and you find another thought - the thought of "me, watching". It arises and passes like the rest. Watch that one too.

Seeing has no seer. When seeing happens, there is just seeing. A thought arising, noticed. Pain occurring, noticed. The noticing itself, noticed. No one stands behind the eyes doing it.

*"I am seeing reality"* is not seeing reality. It is the character claiming a new trophy. The claim is one more thought. Its occurrence is real. Its content is not.

You don't see reality. Seeing happens. It was always happening. The character was just narrating over it.

As soon as you see reality, you are at peace.
