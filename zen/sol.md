# Solution

**See reality.**

Easier said than done. This page addresses the "done" part.

&nbsp;

## Emotional Strength
Emotional Strength is the most important attribute for all humans.

**What**
- Emotional Strength = **How grounded we are amid emotions**.
- With emotional strength, we **feel our emotions fully & take rational actions anyway**.
- Emotions no longer affect our actions.

**Why**
- Once emotions get to us, it's over.
    1. We lose our awareness. We jump from the player seat back into the game as the character.
	2. Then, we lose our ground. Computation plays our character on auto-pilot.
    3. Then, emotions control our actions.

**How**
- **Face difficulties.** It's the only way to cultivate emotional strength.
    - We develop physical strength by exposing our body to difficult physical situations.
    - We develop emotional strength by **exposing our brain to difficult emotional situations**.

&nbsp;

### Avoidance
Emotional Strength ≠ Avoidance.

Avoidance is equivalent to skipping the gym, thus the opposite of emotional strength.

Signs of avoidance:
- Acting indifferent, emotionless, or numb to emotions
- Suppression of negative emotions
- Learned helplessness
- Fear of painful experiences
- Staying in the comfort zone
- Indulging in pleasurable activities
- ...

To cultivate emotional strength, do the opposite:
- Paying extra attention to emotions
- Embracing negative emotions
- Feeling helpless & Taking actions anyway
- Facing painful experiences amid fear
- GTFO of the comfort zone
- Indulging in emotionally painful activities
- ...

&nbsp;

## Awareness

**What**
- Awareness = **Shift attention from illusions to reality**.
- We jump out of the game and recognize we are the player, not the character.
	- The character is an illusion controlled by computation.
	- The player is the **process** of seeing reality, making informed decisions, and taking optimal actions.

**Why**
- As long as the brain stays in the "main character story", it's over.
    1. On auto-pilot, computation traps the brain in illusions.
    2. The brain believes it's the character in the game. It synthesizes various thoughts & emotions around this self identity.
    3. Then, self-attachment causes all problems.

**How**
1. Notice all thoughts & emotions right now.
2. Recognize all of them originate from the self character, an illusion.
3. Recognize none of them has a true self identity. Computation computed them.
4. Shift attention back to reality.

&nbsp;

### Resistance

Awareness ≠ Resistance.

Resistance is still a part of the illusion. Another character is created to battle against the original character. Both illusions.

When resistance occurs, notice it. Recognize it. Back to reality.

&nbsp;

## Frame
Emotional strength means how grounded we are amid emotions.

Where do we ground ourselves? Frame.

**What**
- Frame = **How we play the game**. (like **policy** in RL)
- Lack of frame = let computation play the game on auto-pilot.
- Define our play style however we like, but do NOT let computation dictate our gameplay.
    - You can. Then you watch a movie without realizing you are watching.

**Why**
- The game is here despite being an illusion. Unless we opt out of survival, it is inevitable for us to play the game.
- The character is an illusion. Only the gameplay is real. Each action is computation.

**How**
- **Focus on the PROCESS & ACTIONS.** Only the gameplay is real.
- Any objective or outcome is an illusion.
- Attachment to outcomes = Attachment to the character = Trapped in illusions.

&nbsp;

### Character

Frame ≠ Character.

Self improvement is masturbation. Both exacerbate attachments to illusions.

There's no self character to build or improve.

Frame is the playstyle.
