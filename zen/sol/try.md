# Don't Try

## What
"Don't try" = **Take actions with no outcome expectation**.

2 components:
- **Outcome independence**: Whatever happens happens.
- **Take actions**: We still do what we want to do.

&nbsp;

## Why
Once we "try", it's over.

Trying means we really want to steer reality toward our desired state.

Trying implies 2 things:
- **Attachment** - but the outcome only affects the character, not our player status.
- **Control** - but we can only control causes. Computation controls conditions.
