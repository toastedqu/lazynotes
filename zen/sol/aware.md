# Awareness

## What
We **shift attention from illusions to reality**.

We jump out of the game and recognize we are the player, not the character.

The character is an illusion controlled by computation.

The player is the **process** of seeing reality, making informed decisions, and taking optimal actions.

&nbsp;

## Why
When on auto-pilot, computation traps the brain in illusions.

The brain believes it's the character in the game. It synthesizes various thoughts & emotions around this self identity.

Then this self-attachment causes all problems.

As long as the brain stays there, it's over.

&nbsp;

## How
1. Recognize all ongoing thoughts & emotions.
2. Recognize all of them are centered around the illusory character.
3. Recognize none of them has a true self identity. They naturally emerge from the brain due to computation.
4. Recognize we are the player (i.e., computation), not the character.
5. Shift attention out of the illusions.
