# 8. Metacognition Levels

Suppose a high-risk high-return desire has not been fulfilled for months. Your brain is deeply obsessed with it. Then, a negative event occurs which pushes the desired outcome further away.

For example, she started dating your best friend.

---

&nbsp;

## Jester

### Level 1 - Instinct

You rage. You cry. You throw a tantrum. You cannot think at all. You are fully possessed by impulsion.

&nbsp;

### Level 2 - Ego

You find the situation personal. Your ego is shattered. You scold at the entire world. You become defensive to protect your ego - *"I don't want it anyway!"* You also become offensive to protect your ego - *"WHY NOT ME?! WHAT'S WRONG WITH ME?! WHY DON'T I GET WHAT I WANT?!"*

---

&nbsp;

## Thinker

### Level 3 - Logic

You are deeply hurt, but you understand things happen for a reason. You analyze the event from your own POV to figure out the "what / why / how". *"Maybe these actions I took lowered my odds to attract her" "But I did A/B/C under these conditions which should make her like me." "But..." "Wait..." "idk why this is happening..."* You've wasted precious time and brain cells but cannot draw a deterministic conclusion due to information asymmetry. Your obsession with logic prevents you from processing your emotions.

&nbsp;

### Level 4 - Rationality

You inspect the event holistically by looking at the POV of every involved party. You realize she never liked you from the start. She used you for attention and validation with minimal investment. Worse, she manipulated you in order to get closer to your best friend. You couldn't see it because you built a mental image based on the limited information you have about her. Then, you got attached too early and fell for her breadcrumbs. Every interaction you had with them makes sense now. You now sit with your emotions - anger, depression, resentment, etc.

&nbsp;

### Level 5 - Metacognition

You know what's happening, but you are also able to stand in the shoes of every involved party and understand where they came from at an emotional level. She grew up without proper parental love. She suffered from horrible dating experiences. She has a fucked-up attachment style which resulted in her toxic manipulations. You also understand where you came from. You cannot choose to fall in love. Your attraction came from factors completely out of your control - biology, preferences developed throughout past experiences, whatever. Her breadcrumbing kept you on the hook because of intermittent reinforcement & a lack of better options. You learn the lessons, take some time & space to process your emotions, notify your best friend about her behavioral patterns, but still wish them well.

---

&nbsp;

## Winner

### Level 6 - Frame

No inspection. You have a life to live. You failed a side quest, so there's no point wasting more time and energy on it. It stings though because you liked her after all, so you process these emotions naturally when they come up. You move on.

&nbsp;

### Level 7 - Clarity

You see life as a hyperreality show where computation is the sole actor. You see this event. You see your emotions and thoughts. You see everything clearly, for everything is computation.

---

&nbsp;

## Fucker

### Level 8 - Zen

She started dating your best friend.

*"ok."*
