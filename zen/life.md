# Life

If computation is the true reality and everything is an illusion, then what remains?

**Experience**.

&nbsp;

## New Objective - Experience
See, computation is a dynamic process by nature. Life is a dynamic process by nature.

Nothing in human perception exists. Sure. But we can still **EXPERIENCE** things with our 5 aggregates & 6 senses.

*"But... They are all just illusions..."*

Exactly. So just watch the movie. Or better, act in the movie. Play the game. Experience the game.

You will still feel pain & pleasure. You will still experience ups & downs.

*"So what the fuck is everything up to this point then?"*

Watch the difference. Up till now, you've NEVER actively experienced life. Computation experienced it for you. You were on autopilot, forced to optimize perceived survival & reproduction, passively. Computation dictates everything.

Now? Computation still dictates everything, but you get to actively experience it via one single option - **intentional action**.

&nbsp;

## New Optimization - Intentional Action
Computation is state transition. To trigger a transition from State A to State B, there must be an **action**.

If no button is pressed, the underlying computation (i.e., dependent origination) takes place & passively completes the transition process.

HOWEVER, if a button is actively pressed, a new transition takes place. State A gets transitioned to State C instead.

You didn't see reality before, and you were guided by dependent origination. You see reality now, so you make intentional actions.

Remember the following rules:

### 1. You can ONLY control your current action.
Relinquish control for everything, everyone, every event, every outcome, all emotions, all thoughts, your body, your perception, the past, the future, every fucking thing.

You can only control your current action given the current state.

### 2. You reap what you sow.
Every action choice you make causes state transition, which causes changes in the environment. The environment returns new signals to update your neural network, which affects your future action choices.

This is the true definition of **karma** - the inertia from consistent action choices.

Be careful what action choice to make.

### 3. Your life experience is a sum of your past actions.
If you expand the temporal scale of Rule #2 to your entire life since birth, then you get Rule #3.

### 4. Never take life seriously.
Computation dictates everything. You effectively can't do much other than theoretically control your own action.

Theoretically, yes. Realistically, there's no "self", so even the experience of "controlling your own action" is created by dependent origination.

Knowing that every illusion you perceive & experience is dictated by the underlying computation of the universe, you really shouldn't take life seriously.

## Equipment
To experience the life game to the fullest, I recommend the following equipment for you.

### Emotional Strength
**Emotional Strength = How grounded we are amid emotions.** With emotional strength, we feel our emotions fully & take rational actions anyway. Emotions no longer affect our actions.

Emotional Strength is the SINGLE MOST OVERPOWERED attribute for ALL humans.

Once emotions get to us, it’s over.
1. We lose our awareness. We jump from the player seat back into the game as the NPC controlled by computation.
2. Then, we lose our ground. Computation plays our character on auto-pilot.
3. Then, emotions dictate our actions.

In reality, the only thing impeding us from taking rational actions in any scenario is emotions.

Emotional strength essentially says, "you can take rational actions whenever you are, wherever you are, regardless of how you feel." That's why it is ridiculously overpowered.

Note: Emotional strength is the polar opposite of suppression or avoidance. The latter avoids pain fully, whereas the former faces pain fully.

**Understand emotions.**
- Computation controls emotions. You don't control them. You cannot magically force yourself to become happy or calm.
- Emotions are impermanent. Emotions will go away. Don't mistake temporary emotions as deterministic reality.
- Emotions are not you. They are merely a part of your 5 aggregates, as much as they trick you to believe that's what you are.

**Face difficulties.**
- We develop physical strength by exposing our body to difficult physical situations.
- We develop emotional strength by exposing our brain to difficult emotional situations.

&nbsp;

### Awareness
**Awareness = Shift attention from illusions to reality.** We jump out of the game and recognize we are the player, NOT the character.
- The character is an illusion controlled by the underlying computation.
- The player is intentional action - see reality, make informed decisions, take optimal actions.

As long as the brain stays in the “main character story”, it’s over.
- On auto-pilot, computation traps the brain in illusions.
- The brain believes it’s the character in the game. It synthesizes various thoughts & emotions around this self identity.
- Then, self-attachment causes all problems specified in the previous chapter.

Awareness requires emotional strength to work well. Once emotional strength is mastered, practice awareness by:
1. Notice all thoughts & emotions right now.
2. Recognize you have no control over any of them. They originate from the "self" character controlled by computation.
3. Recognize you can only control your current action.
4. Shift attention back.

&nbsp;

### Frame
**Frame = How we play the game,** like the policy concept in Reinforcement Learning.

Emotional strength means how grounded we are amid emotions. Awareness shifts attention back. Where do we ground ourselves in? Where do we shift attention back to? Frame. If you lack frame, you let computation play the game on autopilot.

No matter how you play the game, **play FOR THE SAKE OF PLAYING. No expectation. No desire. Just play.** Focus on the PROCESS & ACTIONS. Only the gameplay is real. **The only objective is EXPERIENCE.**
