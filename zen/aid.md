# 7. First Aid

NOTE: **Nothing works without emotional strength.**

## ok

Try to say "ok" to everything. The event, the desire, the thoughts, the emotions, the pleasure, the pain, everything.

Whatever happens, say "ok". And if something happens because of the "ok", say "ok" to that too.

&nbsp;

## lifetime breathing

Try to intentionally breathe, whenever you can, wherever you can.

Breathe in. Breathe out. Breathe in. Breathe out. Breathe in. Breathe out.

So that you are ALWAYS meditating. So that you are ALWAYS in reality.

Do this for the rest of your life.
