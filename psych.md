# Psychology

Study notes from [*Thinking, Fast and Slow*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thinking,_Fast_and_Slow) by Daniel Kahneman.

## Thinking
### 2 Systems
- **System 1**: Fast, automatic thinking generates impressions, associations, and intuitive answers.
- **System 2**: Deliberate thinking requires attention & supports reasoning and conscious choices.
- **First impression**: People often accept their first impressions w/o System 2. Automatic impressions & feelings have a strong influence on conscious beliefs & decisions.
- **Familiarity**: Familiar words & associations can enter awareness w/o System 2.
- **Competing responses**: System 1 responses can interfere with System 2 responses, causing worse performance.

### Attention
- **Limited attention**: People have limited attention.
- **Attention overestimation**: People overestimate their ability to notice things. Concentration makes people miss obvious details.
- **Multitasking**: Multitasking is feasible only in System 1.
- **Surprise**: Noticed surprises trigger System 2 more often.
- **Task switching**: Switching between tasks requires effort & diverts attention.
- **Mental load**: When System 2 is occupied, System 1 has strong influence on choices & judgments.
- **Overload reduction**: Divide & Conquer reduces mental overload.
- **Flow**: Deep concentration in one activity makes concentration feel effortless.
- **Attention control**: Attention control is on par with intelligence as a key factor for task performance.

### Bias
- **Intuitive bias**: System 1 is biased & irrational.
- **Belief bias**: People willingly accept what they already believe, even if it's false.
- **Persistent illusions**: Knowing an impression is false does NOT stop it from feeling true.
- **Cognitive illusions**: Misleading impressions occur anywhere in the brain.
- **Error correction**: Recognizing errors helps people suspect cognitive illusions.
- **Judging others**: People recognize others' errors much more easily than their own.
- **Intelligence means nothing**: High intelligence does not mean freedom from bias, because bias comes from System 1.

### Effort
- **Limited capacity**: Different System 2 activities compete for limited mental resources.
- **Repetition**: Prolonged practice makes effortful skills effortless.
- **Least effort**: People tend to choose the minimal effort way to achieve a goal, even at the cost of effectiveness.
- **Pupil dilation**: Pupils widen as mental effort increases & shrink when effort ends.
- **Challenge**: Difficult challenges trigger System 2 more often.

### Control
- **Self-control**: System 2 can suppress System 1.
- **Weakened control**: Alcohol, poor sleep, irregular schedule, and a lack of glucose weakens self-control.
- **Ego depletion**: Exercising self-control reduces ability to exert it later.
- **Motivation**: Strong incentives counter ego depletion.
- **Delaying gratification**: Directing attention away from temptations help delay gratification.
