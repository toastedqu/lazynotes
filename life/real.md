# Reality

Computation is the only reality of the observable universe.

&nbsp;

## Computation
Prerequisites:
- **Phenomenon** : Any mental object or unit of existence.
- **Attribute**: A perceivable, identifiable, distinguishable feature of a phenomenon.
- **State**: A set of attribute values of all phenomena in an arbitrary temporal-spatial scale at an arbitrary time point.

Definition:
- Computation = **state transition**.
    - Buddhism dissects state into **cause/effect** & **conditions**.
    - Computation is the action (i.e., karma) that steers a cause into an effect based on the current set of conditions.
    - I personally believe state transition is simpler and more generalizable.

Scope:
- Computation operates at **any temporal-spatial scale**.
    - It can be as small as a quark, as large as the observable universe, as short as the Planck time, as long as the cosmological decade.

```{note} Example
Flower is a **phenomenon**.

The color, shape, odor of a flower are **attributes**.

When a white lily blooms, its physical **state** consists of 6 white petals, 6 stamens with (dark) red anther & light green filament, and 1 pistil.

A lily bud's tightly furled, green state transitions into an open bloom. Days after, the white petals becomes brown, then falls off. The lily dies. This process is **state transition**.

The **temporal scale** is the lily's full lifespan. The **spatial scale** is the lily's physical body.
```

## Properties
### Impermanence
- Computation = change.
- Change is a dynamic process.
- Every state is dying, and every state is emerging.
- Hence, **no state represents reality**.

```{note} Example
A flower never "began" on its own. Sunlight pours energy into a seed. Soil, rain, air, bacteria, and a bee that pollinated some parent plant months ago all converge at one point in spacetime, and we call that convergence "a flower". It is just a node where countless threads of the computation network briefly cross.

Then, a human cuts it, a florist prices it, packs it, ships it, sells it, and the instant money changes hands the flower stops being mere biology and becomes a symbol: love at a wedding, grief at a funeral, an apology, a birthday. That meaning exists nowhere in the petals. It lives only in human minds, assigned from outside.

The flower sits in a vase, the assigned meaning fades as attention drifts elsewhere, the cells lose water, it wilts, decomposes, and returns to the soil, which becomes the condition for the next seed.

"Birth" and "death" are just two more labels stamped onto one continuous, dependent process. Nothing was ever truly created or destroyed. The network only rearranged itself.
```

&nbsp;

### Unity
- Every phenomenon is dependent on each other, either directly or indirectly.
- No phenomenon can exist with a self identity separable from other phenomena.
- The observable universe is merely a united computational process.
- Hence, **no phenomenon exists in reality**.

```{note} Example
Take the flower apart and ask "where is the flower?"

Pull off the petals, the stamen, the pistil, the sepals, the stem, the leaves. Point to any one piece: is that the flower? No. So keep dissecting.

Each petal is just cells. Each cell is organelles, water, and proteins. Each protein is a chain of molecules. Each molecule is atoms. Each atom is a nucleus and electrons, then quarks and fields and probability.

At no layer do you ever find a hidden "flower-essence" sitting inside.

The "flower" was only ever a label we painted over a temporary arrangement of parts that are themselves arrangements of parts - labels all the way down, with no self-standing object at the bottom.
```

&nbsp;

### Neutrality
- All dualistic labels are human concepts created & reinforced by computation throughout evolution due to survival instinct.
- Computation just is. It has no dualistic label by nature.
- Hence, **no attribute exists in reality**.

```{note} Example
We slice the world of flowers into neat dualities: a red rose means passion, a white lily means death, a chrysanthemum is celebratory in one culture and funereal in another, a "weed" is just a flower someone decided was unwanted. Beautiful versus ugly, lucky versus unlucky, sacred versus ordinary - none of these pairs is found anywhere in the flower itself.

Scan the cells, the pigments, the wavelengths of reflected light: you will find chemistry, but not "passion", not "mourning", not "good" or "bad". The opposites live entirely on our side of the network, projected outward and then mistaken for properties of the thing.

And every label only has meaning by leaning on its opposite - "beautiful" is empty without "ugly", "sacred" needs "profane" to mean anything, so the dualities don't even stand on their own - they arise together, define each other, and dissolve together. Strip the projections away and there is no red-rose-passion and no white-lily-grief, no two opposing things at all - just one undivided process the network is running, which we then carve into pairs purely for our own convenience.
```
