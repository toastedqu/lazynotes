## Theory
> See reality.

That's it. Just see reality.

As soon as you see reality, you are at peace.

1. Did your mind react to this sentence negatively? Finding me arrogant and ignorant? Finding this sentence absurd and funny? Thinking it's so much easier said than done? Thinking I'm ignoring all the people who suffer from severe mental issues? Thinking of the meme "just be happy"?
2. Watch it closely. Who's saying all these shits in your mind? Did "you" "choose" to react this way? Or did these thoughts just emerge naturally as a reaction? If so where did they emerge from?
3. How do the thoughts sound like? Did you paint any mental image inside your mind? If so how does it look like?
4. How do the thoughts feel like? Did you get angry? If so how angry? Do you feel it anywhere in your body? How does it feel like physically?

6. **This, is reality.**

As soon as you see reality, you are at peace.

&nbsp;

Let me use "pain" to represent all negative experiences in the mind.

**"Peace" does NOT mean "painless".** Absolutely not. Here's why:
1. Your mind is a bullshit machine fostered by computation through centuries of evolution based on survival instinct.
2. When you feel pain, you feel pain. The pain is here. This is reality.
3. Whatever bullshit your mind adds on top of it, is also reality.
4. If you overthink a lot, whatever bullshit your mind adds on top of the already generated bullshits, is also reality.
5. "Peace" means, "**The pain is here. Ok. My mind is adding these and those bullshits on top of it. Ok.**"
6. Watch the difference - **The occurrence of the bullshits IS real. The content of the bullshits IS NOT real**, because it is infected with human concepts, which are intrinsically illusory and nonexistent.
    - When you give a fuck about the content of the bullshits, you trap yourself in illusions.
    - However, if you just give a fuck about the occurrence of the bullshits, you see reality.

As soon as you see reality, you are at peace.

## Practice
### Don't take life seriously.
Life experience is an illusion dictated by computation.

Even if you inject "meaning" into certain aspects of the illusion, they will die anyway. It will die anyway. You will die anyway. Memento Mori.

When you take nothing seriously, you enjoy everything automatically.

If you truly embody this law, you can stop here, sit back & relax, and enjoy the movie.

If you want to get something out of this illusion, keep reading.

&nbsp;

### You need emotional strength.
Emotional strength is the ONLY cheat code you need in this game.

You've been fed countless quotes from countless sources countless times. Your brain already knows what to do to maximize your long-term utility for survival on a rational level.

But when it comes to execution, you forget them all.

Why? **Emotions**. They consistently steer you away from awareness and clarity.

As soon as emotions get to you, it's ALREADY over. 

You can memorize all quotes or philosophies or whatever. It won't matter. The more you are controlled by emotions, the more you are on autopilot, the less likely you can get anything out of this game.

However, if you've mastered emotional strength, you ALWAYS win. 

Emotions arise. You see them clearly. You choose to do absolutely nothing about them. No emotion can control you. No one can get to you. Nothing can knock you down. It's quite literally hacking.

The good news is it's not an innate skill, so everyone can build it.

The bad news is it's EXTREMELY DIFFICULT to build. The difficulty lies not in actions, but in repetitions.

To build physical strength, you go to the gym, do exercises that challenge your current physical strength, take rests, then repeat.

To build emotional strength, you **expose yourself to difficulties**, take actions that challenge your current emotional strength, **bounce back**, then **repeat**.

It's extremely hard, but if you don't want emotions to fuck you up again and again and again, there's no other way. The few people with true emotional strength built it involuntarily through extreme hardships in life.

Build emotional strength, or the rest don't matter.

&nbsp;

### You reap what you sow.
You take an action, you cause an effect. That's the ONLY rule of the game.

Depending on the uncontrollable external conditions, the effect can change in **magnitude**, but never in **direction**.

Regardless of the conditions you are in, there's ALWAYS an optimal action which maximizes your benefits or minimizes your losses.

Think about your action before execution.

&nbsp;

### You are dying.
Time is the single most important resource in this game.

You CANNOT replenish time. You are ALWAYS spending it involuntarily. You NEVER know how much time you have left. You are dying. You will die.

Prioritize time management.

&nbsp;

### You own nothing.
Change & Death are the only 2 certainties in this game.

Everything is changing and everything is dying. Everything comes and goes. You own it now, you lose it later. You own it for the rest of your life, you lose it at your death bed.

"At least I own it for my lifetime!" Sure. You have expectations of your "possession", then it changes beyond your expectations, so you adjust your expectations to "fix the ownership", then it changes again, so you adjust again, then it changes again, so you adjust again, ... You've wasted tons of time and energy, then you lose it anyway.

Be prepared to lose everything.

&nbsp;

You are a player in this game. You want to get something out of it. You define your own objectives and tasks. You define what "winning" means to you.

It doesn't matter what you want out of the game. The progression of your story is dependent on you, the main character. If you don't progress, the story cannot progress. If you are not on par with what you want, you cannot get it. Plain and simple.

Think of any multiplayer game you've played. Your satisfaction from it is purely dependent on how good you are at it. Same as this game of life.

Build yourself.
