# 5. Acceptance

You now know everything you perceive is an illusion dictated by computation.

You now know you are an illusion trapped inside the survival game managed by computation.

In the illusory realm, you are born to die. You live an ephemeral dream, and you die.

In the illusory realm, everything you give a fuck about will die.

What should you do about it?

You should do absolutely nothing about it.

You accept reality, and you go about your day.

You can do whatever you want.

If you want to have a more satisfying life experience, you can practice frame control, emotional awareness, and emotional strength.

If you have something you want to do, you can take consistent actions toward it.

Because in the end, **nothing matters**, for there is not a thing in reality.

There is only computation. Action. Process. Continuum. Karma. God's plan. Whatever the fuck you call it.

Everything else? Illusion.

**Accept everything.**

**Take nothing seriously.**

**Have fun.**
